Overview

Contract Bridge is one of the most intellectually demanding card games ever devised. Two partnerships compete to win tricks, with an elaborate bidding system that communicates information between partners. It has been a competitive mind sport for over a century.

Key Concepts

  • Bidding phase determines the contract (tricks to win and trump suit)
  • Declarer plays both their hand and dummy's hand
  • Defenders try to prevent declarer from making the contract
  • Scoring rewards bidding and making contracts accurately
  • Communication through legal bidding conventions is essential
