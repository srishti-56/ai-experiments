Overview

Snap is a fast-paced card game all about reflexes. Players take turns flipping cards onto a central pile. When two consecutive cards match in rank, the first player to shout "SNAP!" wins the pile. It's loud, chaotic, and perfect for groups.

How to Play

  • Deal all cards face-down as evenly as possible
  • Players take turns flipping their top card onto a central pile
  • When two consecutive cards share the same rank — shout "SNAP!"
  • First to shout wins the entire central pile
  • If you call Snap incorrectly, you give cards to the other player
  • Player who collects all the cards wins
