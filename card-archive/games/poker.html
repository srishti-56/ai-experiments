Overview

Poker is a family of comparing card games in which players wager over which hand is best according to the game's rules. It combines strategy, probability, and psychology — reading your opponents is just as important as reading your cards.

Hand Rankings

  • Royal Flush — A, K, Q, J, 10 of the same suit
  • Straight Flush — Five consecutive cards of the same suit
  • Four of a Kind — Four cards of the same rank
  • Full House — Three of a kind plus a pair
  • Flush — Five cards of the same suit
  • Straight — Five consecutive cards
  • Three of a Kind — Three cards of the same rank
  • Two Pair — Two different pairs
  • One Pair — Two cards of the same rank
  • High Card — No combination, highest card plays
