Overview

UNO is one of the world's best-selling card games. With its custom deck of colored and numbered cards plus action cards, UNO is easy to learn but creates intense moments — especially when someone stacks Draw Fours or forgets to say "UNO!"

How to Play

  • Deal 7 cards to each player
  • Match the top discard by color, number, or symbol
  • Action cards: Skip, Reverse, Draw Two
  • Wild cards can be played anytime — choose the next color
  • Wild Draw Four forces the next player to draw 4 cards
  • Say "UNO!" when you have one card left or draw 2 as penalty
