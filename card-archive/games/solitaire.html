Overview

Solitaire (Klondike) is the world's most popular single-player card game. The goal is to move all 52 cards to four foundation piles, built up by suit from Ace to King. It became a cultural icon through its inclusion in early Windows operating systems.

How to Play

  • Deal 28 cards into 7 tableau columns (1, 2, 3... 7 cards)
  • Only the top card of each column is face-up
  • Build tableau stacks in descending order, alternating colors
  • Move Aces to foundation piles and build up by suit
  • Draw from the stock pile when no moves are available
  • Win by moving all cards to the four foundation piles
