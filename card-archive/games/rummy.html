Overview

Rummy is a group of matching-card games built around forming sets (cards of the same rank) and runs (consecutive cards of the same suit). Players draw and discard cards strategically, trying to be the first to "go out" by melding all their cards.

Basic Rules

  • Draw a card from the stock or discard pile each turn
  • Form melds: sets of 3+ same-rank cards or runs of 3+ sequential suited cards
  • Lay down melds when ready, or add to existing melds
  • Discard one card to end your turn
  • First player to meld all cards wins the round
  • Remaining players score penalty points for cards left in hand
