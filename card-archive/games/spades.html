Spades

Bid and take tricks with a trump

Overview

Spades is a partnership trick-taking game where spades are always trump. Before play begins, each player bids the number of tricks they expect to take. Accuracy matters — overbidding and underbidding both carry penalties. Teamwork and card counting are key.

How to Play

  • Deal all 52 cards evenly (13 per player)
  • Each player bids the number of tricks they will take
  • Partnership bids are combined into a team bid
  • Follow suit if possible; spades are always trump
  • Making your bid scores 10 points per trick bid
  • Overtricks ("bags") count as 1 point but 10 bags = -100 penalty
