Overview

War is pure luck — no decisions, no strategy, just the thrill of the flip. It's often the first card game children learn and remains a surprisingly satisfying way to pass time. The entire deck is split between two players who simultaneously reveal cards.

How to Play

  • Split the deck evenly between two players
  • Both players flip their top card simultaneously
  • Higher card wins both cards (Ace is highest)
  • On a tie: place 3 cards face-down, flip a 4th — that's "War"
  • Winner of War takes all cards on the table
  • Game ends when one player has all 52 cards
