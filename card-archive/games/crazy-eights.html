Crazy Eights

Match suit or rank to shed cards

At a Glance

Players
2 – 7
Deck
52 Cards
Type
Shedding
Skill Level
Low

Overview

Crazy Eights is the original card-shedding game that inspired UNO. Players take turns matching the top discard by suit or rank. Eights are wild — play them anytime and choose the next suit. First to empty their hand wins.

How to Play

  • Deal 5 cards each (or 7 for 2 players)
  • Flip one card from the stock to start the discard pile
  • Play a card matching the top card's suit or rank
  • Eights are wild — play anytime and name the new suit
  • Draw from the stock if you can't play
  • First player to empty their hand wins the round
