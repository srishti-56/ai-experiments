Overview

Blackjack (also known as 21) is the most widely played casino card game in the world. The goal is simple: get a hand value as close to 21 as possible without going over, while beating the dealer's hand.

Basic Rules

  • Number cards are worth their face value
  • Face cards (J, Q, K) are worth 10
  • Aces are worth 1 or 11
  • Hit to draw another card, Stand to keep your hand
  • Blackjack (Ace + 10-value) pays 3:2
  • Bust over 21 and you lose immediately
