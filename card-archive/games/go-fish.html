Overview

Go Fish is a beloved children's card game about collecting sets of four matching cards. Players ask opponents for specific ranks — if they have it, they hand it over. If not: "Go Fish!" Draw from the pond and hope for the best.

How to Play

  • Deal 5 cards to each player (7 for 2 players)
  • On your turn, ask any player for a specific rank you hold
  • If they have it, they give you all cards of that rank
  • If not, they say "Go Fish" and you draw from the pool
  • When you collect all 4 of a rank, lay them down as a set
  • Player with the most sets when all cards are matched wins
