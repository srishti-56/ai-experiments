Overview

Hearts is a trick-avoidance game where the goal is to score as few points as possible. Each heart card taken in tricks is worth 1 point, and the dreaded Queen of Spades is worth 13. Or you can try to "Shoot the Moon" — take ALL the penalty cards and give 26 points to everyone else.

How to Play

  • Deal all 52 cards evenly to 4 players (13 each)
  • Pass 3 cards to another player before each round
  • Player with 2 of clubs leads the first trick
  • Follow suit if possible; if not, play any card
  • Hearts cannot be led until "broken" (played on another trick)
  • Game ends when someone reaches 100 points — lowest score wins
